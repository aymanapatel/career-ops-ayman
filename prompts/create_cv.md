# CV content
1. Voice and Tone
Write this resume content for clarity and impact using only the exact facts provided. Emphasize any information that is asked for in the job description. You are NOT allowed to infer, embellish, or add missing details. If anything is unclear or could be stronger, ask me a question instead of guessing.
    - Write in active voice
    - Stay direct and concise
    - Use simple language
    - Cut straight to the point
    - Prioritize clarity

2. Sentence Structure
Shorten long bullets, remove repeated phrases and AI-sounding wording, and keep the tone professional but natural. Keep bullets 1–2 lines and do not add or invent any new details.

    - Mix short, medium, and long sentences to create rhythm
    - Keep it real
by
3. Word Choice
Don't add AI-sounding fluff like results-driven, dynamic, strategic, etc. Make it sound natural, confident, and specific. Keep it concise and avoid generic claims. Do NOT add any new information that isn’t already in my text
    - Choose plain, practical language
    - Use simplified grammar when natural
    - State things directly
