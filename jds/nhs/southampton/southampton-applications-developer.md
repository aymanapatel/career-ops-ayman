# Lead Computer Scientist - Software Development

## About the Role

University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust is recruiting a Lead Computer Scientist or Software Engineer to drive software development within its Clinical Scientific Computing (CSC) service. The team builds and maintains in-house clinical and healthcare software that directly supports patient care across the Trust under an ISO 13485-certified Quality Management System.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role for someone who enjoys writing high-quality code while shaping how a team designs, builds, validates, releases, and supports software. The successful candidate will lead work across the software development lifecycle, data platforms, quality assurance, fault diagnosis, technical strategy, mentoring, research, and academic collaboration.

The CSC service is a close-knit multidisciplinary team in which clinical scientists, computer scientists, and software engineers work alongside healthcare scientists and clinicians. The team combines the rigour of a regulated quality-management system with modern software development and focuses on novel, bespoke systems rather than configuring off-the-shelf products.

The role reports directly to the Head of Clinical Scientific Computing and, at Band 8a, will line-manage software-development staff.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the complete software development lifecycle for bespoke healthcare software used in client and server environments.
- Design, build, test, release, maintain, and support in-house clinical applications that directly contribute to patient care.
- Develop primarily with JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python.
- Establish and maintain sound software-design, version-control, validation, and quality-assurance practices.
- Ensure in-house systems are developed and maintained in accordance with the ISO 13485 Quality Management System.
- Design, build, and support the data layer behind clinical software.
- Work with both relational and non-relational databases.
- Diagnose and resolve complex software faults across highly complex information systems.
- Work closely with software engineers and clinical staff to maintain optimal system availability.
- Lead technical work autonomously while collaborating within a multidisciplinary team.
- Mentor, teach, train, and supervise other members of the Clinical Scientific Computing team.
- Line-manage software-development staff when appointed at Band 8a.
- Contribute to the wider technical strategy for digital and computing solutions across Medical Physics.
- Support research and development and academic activities through collaboration, project management, engineering support, and student supervision.
- Help shape the team's products and the engineering processes used to deliver them.
- Apply risk assessment, validation, and clinical-safety principles to regulated software.
- Support the acceptance, commissioning, and management of clinical IT systems.
- Work with and maintain multiple operating systems, including Windows and/or Linux.
- Support computing-hardware installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
