# Applications Developer

## About the Role

The Applications Developer builds, tests, supports, and maintains bespoke web and mobile applications that improve how information is processed and shared across Midlands Partnership University NHS Foundation Trust (MPFT). The role works with stakeholders to identify service improvements, document expected benefits, and deliver reliable, reusable applications and services.

The developer works primarily with the Trust's core stack: .NET/C#, SQL Server, React, React Native, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and Tailwind. The role also supports Microsoft Power Platform, SharePoint, IIS, Azure services, APIs, integration points, production operations, and approved AI-enabled workflows and Model Context Protocol (MCP) patterns.

The role is self-managed under the supervision of the Principal Developer (Applications). It is expected to grow in autonomy, support increasingly complex changes, and provide day-to-day coaching, task guidance, peer review, and knowledge sharing for junior team members or trainees.

## Responsibilities

- Specialise in application development and work with stakeholders to identify opportunities for application improvements, automation, and AI-assisted workflow enhancements.
- Develop application features from agreed specifications using .NET/C#, SQL Server, React, React Native, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, Tailwind, Microsoft Power Platform, SharePoint, IIS, and Azure services.
- Build, test, and maintain bespoke web and mobile applications.
- Support the delivery and maintenance of application services under the direction of the Principal Developer (Applications).
- Support SharePoint workflows, pages, sites, and content updates where required.
- Write, amend, refactor, and document application code using modern tooling and technologies.
- Operate and maintain production applications and act as first-line support for application issues.
- Apply modern UI/UX principles to deliver usable, accessible, and responsive solutions.
- Build and maintain front-end features that follow approved patterns, including the NHS Design System and MPFT Design System.
- Contribute to a shared component library, including its documentation, examples, and versioning, to promote consistency across applications.
- Help assess requirements, document solutions and expected benefits, and prepare specifications.
- Work with stakeholders to identify service-improvement opportunities and improve application features.
- Assess the feasibility of new opportunities and contribute to prioritisation.
- Follow agreed standards and methods and participate in code reviews.
- Keep technical skills current, track technology changes, propose improvements, and contribute to modern, well-engineered solutions.
- Apply security controls and safe information-handling practices; identify risks and mitigations, use secure coding and governance practices, and escalate concerns appropriately.
- Support and maintain live applications, troubleshoot with modern tools, and deploy releases and environment changes through agreed CI/CD processes.
- Develop and run tests to validate application changes.
- Monitor and resolve application defects and performance issues.
- Estimate effort and contribute to planning under the agreed delivery methodology.
- Produce Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), runbooks, technical documentation, release notes, user-facing guidance, and adoption or training material where required.
- Support Change Advisory Board (CAB) activities by collating, tracking, and presenting assurance evidence where required.
- Prepare project plans and status reports for assigned workstreams in line with internal processes.
- Work closely with the Principal Developer (Applications) on features, deployment schedules, service impacts, and expected benefits.
- Communicate clearly with stakeholders and project teams, tailoring technical content to the audience.
- Track delivery against targets, flag issues early, and help adjust plans to preserve quality and meet required timeframes.
- Contribute to the reliable day-to-day operation of the application portfolio and supporting services.
- Deliver changes of increasing complexity with greater autonomy over time and support knowledge sharing within the team.
- Build and maintain APIs and integration points, working closely with the Integration & Automation team where required.
- Routinely monitor application availability, errors, and performance; escalate issues and contribute to incident and problem resolution.
- Maintain accurate application documentation to support reliable operations and change control.
- Use AI-assisted development to improve delivery speed and quality while maintaining human oversight through peer review, testing, documentation, and governance.
- Deliver AI-enabled application features using approved AI services and MCP patterns, ensuring traceability, safe information handling, and compliance with governance, security, and clinical-safety requirements.
- Provide day-to-day coaching and support to junior team members or trainees, including task guidance, peer review, and sharing good application-development practices.
- Understand the team's software and hardware requirements and advise management when deficiencies impede productivity.
- Proactively undertake training and development to improve proficiency in the Trust's core stack and tooling.
- Share technical knowledge with team members and users where appropriate.
- Work cooperatively with stakeholders to determine how applications can support service needs and improve ways of working.
- Act independently within guidelines and decide when matters need referral to senior colleagues.
- Make recommendations that account for user needs, technical constraints, security, governance, and clinical safety.
- Investigate issues, find solutions where possible, and follow the change-management process.
- Persuade, negotiate with, and motivate stakeholders on delivery, including links to other initiatives, and communicate sensitive information about performance and change where required.
- Engage mainly with colleagues, with limited exposure to service users.
- Present to audiences of varying sizes where necessary.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Educated to degree level in computer or computing science, or holds an equivalent IT/Digital qualification or equivalent level of experience. Assessed by application and interview.

- Experience with industry-standard coding languages and software-development platforms.

- Experience working with complex datasets, including designing queries and handling data-quality issues.
- Experience designing, developing, and querying large databases such as SQL Server or an equivalent.

- Strong documentation skills for technical notes, support notes, runbooks, and user guidance appropriate to operational support and audit needs.
- Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, including the ability to explain technical topics clearly to non-technical audiences.
- Ability to plan, prioritise, and deliver work to deadlines while working with limited supervision and managing competing demands.
