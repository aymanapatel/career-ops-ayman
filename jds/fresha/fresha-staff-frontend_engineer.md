# Staff Frontend Engineer

## About the Role

Fresha is an AI-powered operating system for the beauty, wellness, and self-care industry. Its platform supports appointment booking, payments, point-of-sale, customer management, marketing automation, loyalty, inventory, and team management for businesses worldwide.

The Staff Frontend Engineer will report to the Head of Engineering for a product tribe and will be responsible for the tribe’s technical vision, architecture, strategy, and execution.

This is a highly technical, hands-on individual-contributor position rather than a people-management role. The engineer will lead major technical initiatives, make architectural decisions, manage technical risks, and improve the stability, scalability, reliability, and performance of Fresha’s business-critical systems.

The role requires close collaboration with Product, Design, Data, Quality Assurance, and engineering teams in a fast-paced and continuously changing environment.

## Responsibilities

* Define and execute the technical vision and strategy for a product tribe.
* Own the architecture of systems within the tribe.
* Remain actively involved in hands-on software development and coding.
* Lead cross-team and cross-tribe technical initiatives.
* Anticipate technical risks and make architectural decisions that prevent future issues.
* Improve the stability, scalability, reliability, and performance of major business-critical systems.
* Ensure engineering projects are delivered on time and to a high standard.
* Lead by example and ensure teams follow established engineering practices.
* Collaborate closely with Product, Design, Data, Quality Assurance, and engineering stakeholders.
* Establish clear technical goals and ensure teams regularly deliver value to users.
* Facilitate continuous learning and improvement across engineering teams.
* Ensure systems meet Fresha’s quality, reliability, and scalability standards.
* Anticipate major technology changes and help Fresha remain ahead of industry developments.
* Make system-wide architectural trade-offs based on business and engineering requirements.
* Understand and communicate the scaling and reliability limitations of major systems.
* Act as an engineering multiplier by building internal systems, tools, policies, and reusable patterns.
* Improve engineering productivity across the organisation.
* Lead internal technical discussions and build consensus around major technology decisions.
* Represent Fresha externally through engineering talks, blog posts, and technical communities.
* Mentor engineers and provide technical guidance across teams.
* Debug complex production and engineering problems.
* Simplify services, software systems, development processes, and architecture.
* Contribute to core engineering projects and supporting technical initiatives.
* Assist engineering teams directly when delivery or operational support is required.
