\textbf{Re: Backend Software Engineer}

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying because this role brings together three areas that have shaped how I work: owning products end to end, sharing what I learn with others, and using AI to improve productivity while keeping outputs predictable and maintainable. Lloyds' focus on secure, resilient microservices, engineering quality and continuous improvement closely matches that approach.

\textbf{End-to-end ownership.} During six years at Mastercard, I worked across APIs, database modelling, frontend delivery, UX, performance, CI/CD, testing and production support. My commercial backend experience is primarily Java and Spring, where I built resilient microservices, event-driven flows and services used by multiple clients. I have also built a Go microservices stack with gRPC, OpenAPI, request validation and Envoy load balancing. Beyond coding, I worked with DevOps on CDN adoption and production deployment scripts, owned releases from lower environments into production, and investigated incidents through Splunk alerts and logs. I also addressed vulnerable dependencies and introduced DOMPurify to sanitise inputs against XSS risks.

\textbf{Sharing what I learn.} I led Mastercard's accessibility work, helped more than ten engineering teams adopt accessible patterns and contributed fixes to the internal design system. I ran regular knowledge-sharing sessions, documented architecture and delivery practices, and explained technical decisions to engineers, product stakeholders and non-specialists. Outside work, I have published more than 40 articles across backend, frontend, databases and AI on my \cvLink{https://aymanace2049.hashnode.dev}{technical blog}.

\textbf{Reliable use of AI.} My academic foundation began with a Bachelor of Computer Science in India. At Queen Mary University of London, my MSc in Advanced Computer Science covered advanced data analytics, machine learning, data engineering and functional programming with Haskell. At Nebius Academy, I built systems involving AI agents, different LLM architectures, MLOps and fine-tuning open-source models. That experience taught me to look beyond the black box and make practical trade-offs around token cost, hallucinations and context management. I use fit-for-purpose models, guardrails and deterministic checks to keep AI-assisted work useful without allowing it to drift into unmaintainable code.

What sets me apart is my willingness to enter any part of a system and drive the work to completion while protecting quality and existing processes. I would bring that ownership, curiosity and practical engineering judgement to Lloyds as you build scalable financial services in Go.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my payments experience, system ownership and approach to reliable engineering could support the team.
