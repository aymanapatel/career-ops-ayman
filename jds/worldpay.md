# Software Engineer - Java

## About the Role

Join Worldpay’s WPG team to develop and support one of the world’s largest global payment platforms by transaction volume.

The role focuses on building robust, secure, high-capacity Java applications for an ecommerce payments gateway. The platform processes more than 800 transactions per second, exceeds 10 million successful payments per day, operates continuously, and targets five-nines availability.

You will work within a cross-functional engineering team containing software engineers, quality assurance engineers, analysts, product owners, and a scrum master. The team owns its services throughout the full software development lifecycle, from initial design through deployment and post-production support.

A background in payments or financial technology would be particularly beneficial.

## Responsibilities

* Design and develop ecommerce payment-gateway solutions using Java, SQL, and PL/SQL.
* Build software for a high-capacity, mission-critical, 24/7 platform targeting five-nines availability.
* Develop solutions that align with product requirements, business value, Worldpay architecture principles, and engineering standards.
* Produce secure, clean, maintainable, and testable distributed applications.
* Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, knowledge sharing, and mentoring.
* Collaborate with software engineers and quality assurance engineers throughout development and testing.
* Work closely with engineering teams based in the United Kingdom and the United States.
* Contribute to retrospectives, continuous improvement initiatives, and technical-debt reduction.
* Follow engineering standards covering code quality, agile delivery, testing, and maintainability.
* Collaborate across the complete software development lifecycle with developers, solution designers, release teams, support teams, analysts, and business stakeholders.
* Refine, release, deploy, operate, and support software in production.
* Provide periodic release and deployment support outside normal office hours.
* Help maintain a fast feedback loop between product, development, testing, and end users.
