# Senior Software Engineer (Backend)

## About the Role

Apron is a B2B AI-native fintech building the ultimate payments powerhouse for UK SMEs. Founded in 2021 and headquartered in London, Apron provides a unified financial operations workspace for accountants, bookkeepers, and business owners — covering invoice capture, bill pay, payroll, expenses, and bookkeeping. The company has raised $49.4M in total funding and employs ~79 people across 9 countries.

As a Senior Software Engineer (Backend), you'll design systems, develop core financial integrations, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to innovate and improve the product experience. You'll work on product verticals like Capture, Bill Pay, Apron Card, GetPaid, and horizontals like FinCrime.

## Responsibilities

- Work cross-functionally with Frontend Engineers, Product Managers, and Product Designers to develop new products and features.
- Design systems and develop core financial integrations to guarantee customer funds are secure and transactions are routed with market-leading speed.
- Share your experience with others, driving innovation and proactively suggesting improvements.
- Help shape the future of Apron's product offerings across multiple financial services verticals.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 6–15 years of continuous experience as a backend engineer or backend-leaning full-stack engineer.
- Experience working in a product-led, fast-paced environment (startup or tech company).
- Experience with relational databases.
- Working knowledge of cloud platforms, with preference for Google Cloud Platform.
- Keen attention to code quality, passion for efficient architecture, and commitment to optimising performance.
- Readiness to work with Kotlin for server-side development (Kotlin proficiency not mandatory, but willingness to learn is essential).

## Preferred Qualifications

- Proficiency in Kotlin for server-side development.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and Terraform.
- Experience with advanced Postgres features like GIST indexes, partitioning, and replication.
