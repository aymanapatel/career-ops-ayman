# Senior Engineer (Servicing)

## About the Role

Funding Circle is looking for a Senior Engineer to join the Loan Servicing team within Product Engineering. This team builds and maintains the core systems responsible for managing the lifecycle of loans after they have been issued.

As a senior engineer, you'll work on evolving critical loan servicing infrastructure, ensuring systems remain accurate, scalable, resilient, and easy to operate. You'll collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, and stakeholders to solve complex backend challenges while helping shape technical direction and engineering best practices.

This role is well-suited for engineers who enjoy working on distributed backend systems, mentoring teammates, and tackling complex business domains with real-world impact.

---

## Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain backend systems powering the loan servicing platform.
- Improve the accuracy, scalability, and reliability of mission-critical financial systems.
- Work across complex backend services and domain models to solve challenging engineering problems.
- Collaborate closely with engineers through pairing and technical discussions.
- Drive technical decisions and contribute to architecture across the servicing platform.
- Proactively identify technical risks, bottlenecks, and system improvements.
- Mentor engineers and help raise the technical capability of the wider team.
- Communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Navigate ambiguity and independently drive solutions from problem definition to implementation.
- Contribute to engineering best practices around testing, reliability, and maintainability.
