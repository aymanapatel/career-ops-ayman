# Software Engineer

## About the Role

Spruce is building software for the UK home energy transition. Its mission is to make it simple and affordable for every home in the UK to be heated and powered by clean, green electricity.

The company serves heat pump installers, who have historically relied on outdated and ineffective software. Since starting at the beginning of 2024, Spruce has raised several funding rounds from top-tier VCs and now supports over 500 heat pump installation businesses across the UK.

Spruce has built an end-to-end operating system for heat pump installers, covering the journey from initial enquiry through design, installation, compliance, and paperwork. Compared with alternatives, Spruce says the platform halves installer time per job, doubles sales conversion rates, and improves quality, margins, and customer experience.

The company is now expanding beyond heat pumps into the wider home renewables supply chain, supporting manufacturers and merchants as well as installers, and enabling solar and battery installations alongside heat pumps.

## Responsibilities

- Work with a small engineering, design, and domain expert team to build and scale the Spruce platform.
- Own and deliver complex product features end to end.
- Shape system architecture and technical direction, depending on experience.
- Mentor other engineers and raise the engineering bar, depending on seniority.
- Work on meaningful production problems with high ownership from day one.
- Build software across a deep product space that blends physical-world constraints with user-facing workflows.
- Work on heat loss modelling and hydronic system design.
- Build 2D and 3D floor-plan tooling using LiDAR and computer vision.
- Support large-scale data ingestion and geospatial analysis.
- Build offline-first, collaborative PWAs.
- Integrate with hardware such as Bluetooth measurement tools, sensors, and monitoring systems.
- Spend time with installers on-site to understand pain points and rapidly suggest and implement solutions based on real feedback.
