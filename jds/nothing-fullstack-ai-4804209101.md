# Full Stack Developer, Software Product (AI)

## About the Company

Nothing exists to make tech feel exciting again. Founded in London in 2020, Nothing has grown from idea to global challenger in just a few years. Backed by GV (Google Ventures), EQT Ventures, C Ventures, and investors like Tony Fadell (iPod), Casey Neistat, and Kevin Lin (Twitch). Now sold in 40+ markets with millions of users worldwide.

Nothing is igniting change in consumer technology. Today, we're left with just a few large incumbents lacking instinct and courage, making cold, unexciting and derivative products. Consumers are bored. Nothing is here to make tech fun again, unleash creativity, and inspire the community.

## About the Role

You'll be joining the Industrial Design team -- a cross-functional group of Industrial Designers, Experience Designers, and Creative Technologists working from the design studio in Kings Cross. This isn't a traditional software development role; you might have a background in front-end engineering, creative coding, physical computing, or something else entirely.

## Responsibilities

- Rapidly prototype and ship novel AI interfaces -- from chat and voice to multi-modal interactions -- that define the next generation of the Nothing OS experience.
- Translate wild, experimental concepts into robust, production-grade full-stack applications using Next.js, React, and Node/Python.
- Optimise the "feel" of AI; obsess over latency, real-time performance, and the micro-interactions that make a digital assistant feel responsive and human.
- Define and architect the underlying patterns and frameworks that power the AI service layer, ensuring experimental code can scale to a global user base.
- Work directly with hardware and OS teams to blur the lines between web, mobile, and embedded surfaces, creating seamless continuity across devices.
- Collaborate with Product Designers to invent entirely new interaction models, moving beyond the traditional screen-tap paradigm.
- Own deployment pipelines and infrastructure choices, ensuring high shipping velocity while maintaining reliability.
- Build and iterate on functional prototypes that define how the product ecosystem behaves -- moving quickly from concept to working experience.
- Partner with the Design team to build new interfaces and experiences, including the APIs and hardware integrations (BLE, Haptics, Sensors) that connect hardware and software.
- Research and de-risk complex features -- providing the evidence that helps the team commit to the right ideas with confidence.

## What We're Looking For

- **Deep Product Pragmatism:** You have a bias toward shipping. A working prototype in a user's hands is worth infinitely more than perfect code on your local machine.
- **Technical Excellence:** Battle-hardened experience with React and Next.js, backed by strong backend skills in Node.js or Python. Comfortable with microservices and cloud-native infrastructure.
- **AI Fluency:** Hands-on experience or deep curiosity for APIs, MCPs, LLMs, and vector databases. Understanding of the unique constraints of building with non-deterministic models.
- **Experience integrating LLMs into products**, whether that's AI-powered prototyping, agent systems, or orchestration. MCP experience is a plus.
- **Deep expertise in at least one programming language** (JavaScript, Kotlin, Swift, or similar). Expected to use AI tools to accelerate work -- but no substitute for genuine fluency.
- **Full-stack experience with strong front-end craft** -- systems thinking and data flow, but also an eye for design, motion, and interaction detail. Great taste.
