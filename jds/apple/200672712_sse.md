# iCloud Platform - Senior Software Engineer

## About the Role

Apple is looking for an experienced full-stack Software Engineer to help build the next generation of cloud services and web applications for developers using iCloud.

The role involves collaborating with cross-functional engineering teams to design and implement frameworks and systems that power iCloud Platform. These systems support products and services such as FaceTime, Push Notifications, Photos, and Backup.

## Responsibilities

* Architect and develop complex web applications.
* Design and implement frameworks and systems for iCloud Platform.
* Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams.
* Lead feature development from conception through production.
* Improve testing, automation, tooling, and development workflows.
* Use generative AI tooling to accelerate delivery while maintaining code quality.
* Build reliable systems capable of supporting existing and future Apple applications.

## Minimum Qualifications

* Comprehensive relevant software engineering experience.
* Proven experience architecting and developing complex web applications.
* Ability to lead feature development from conception to production.
* Excellent problem-solving and analytical thinking skills.
* Strong focus on quality and attention to detail.
* Experience with testing, automation, and developer tooling.
* Experience using generative AI tools in software development.

## Preferred Qualifications

* Strong opinions, loosely held.
* Experience working with cross-functional engineering teams.
* Experience developing large-scale cloud platforms or distributed systems.
* Experience building systems that support high-visibility consumer applications.
