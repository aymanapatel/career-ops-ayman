## REQ-E16 — Motivation to deliver practical outcomes that improve university operations

I have spent six years building and operating integrations that cannot afford to fail. At Mastercard, the systems I owned sat on regulated, high-stakes paths — payment platforms, confidential payment hardware, and event-driven pipelines — where a missed event or a cascading failure had a real, immediate cost. That experience taught me a conviction that drives the kind of work I want to do next: **reliability is not a technical nicety, it is what makes an organisation actually serve the people who depend on it.**

What draws me to Queen Mary specifically is where that skill can be pointed next. Mastercard's output is transactions; a university's output is education and opportunity. The integration platform this role will build connects the systems that keep an institution running — student records, finance, HR and payroll, research administration, library and curriculum, identity, CRM. When those flows work reliably and observably, staff spend less time troubleshooting and more time supporting students; when they fail, every downstream team feels it. I am motivated by engineering that quietly removes friction for a large, human organisation — the kind of platform that makes a university work better without anyone noticing how much effort went into it.

I am also motivated by the platform's purpose and trajectory. Moving from point-to-point integrations to an event-driven backbone with clean producer/consumer separation, change-data-capture pipelines, and Go/Python services is exactly the modern integration pattern I have been practising and writing about — and I want to apply it to an institution whose mission is genuinely public good, not just to another commercial platform. Applying regulated-industry engineering discipline to higher education is, for me, a meaningful next chapter.

**In short:** I want to take the reliability-and-observability discipline I built in payments and use it to make a university's core systems integrate cleanly, so the people who work and study there get better service. That is the practical outcome I am motivated to deliver.
