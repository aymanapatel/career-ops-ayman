## How to use

1. Scan careers links weekly for **Senior Full Stack / Software Engineer / Platform** roles in London/hybrid.  
2. Confirm sponsorship on the JD or ask at first screen.  
3. Prefer Tier A volume + Tier B product fit; use Tier C sparingly.  
4. When a live role looks good → paste URL into career-ops for full evaluation.  
5. Re-verify licence status on gov.uk before investing heavy prep time.
